# College Transport Policy
**Document Type:** POLICY  
**System:** College Bus Tracking System  
**Status:** TEMPLATE — college/transport-office approval required before production use

## 1. Purpose
This document describes general rules for safe and reliable use of the college bus service.

## 2. Route and Schedule Information
- Passengers use the College Bus Tracking System to select a route number and view approved route information.
- The application may display scheduled timings, stops, live bus location, and estimated arrival information when available.
- Live ETA is an estimate, not a guarantee.
- If GPS is stale, unavailable, unreliable, or the bus is off-route, the application must show an appropriate status instead of presenting a false precise ETA.

## 3. Passenger Access
- Students and faculty can use the passenger application without creating an account.
- A passenger manually selects a route number from the available college routes.
- Passenger route selection is not the same as an authenticated passenger identity or roster assignment.
- Passenger-facing screens must not expose private phone numbers, roll numbers, faculty IDs, bus-pass IDs, administrator data, or other restricted information.

## 4. Transport Administration
- Administrators manage routes, stops, schedules, capacity, drivers, passenger rosters, advisors, alerts, issues, emergencies, and operational records.
- Administrative changes to important transport data should be auditable.
- Driver credentials and private contact information are restricted to authorized administrative functions.

## 5. Delayed Bus Information
- The system may identify a route as predicted late when sufficiently reliable ETA evidence indicates that the college-arrival deadline may be missed.
- A predicted late condition is different from confirmed actual late arrival.
- Advisor notifications concern students assigned to the affected route in the approved roster; the system does not infer whether a particular student actually boarded the bus.

## 6. Issue Reporting
- Passengers may submit anonymous transport issue reports.
- Similar reports may be grouped using AI-assisted semantic similarity.
- Grouping does not by itself confirm that an allegation is true.
- Administrators review and manage issues using the original reports as evidence.

## 7. Emergency Reporting
- Passengers can report a breakdown or accident without an account.
- A passenger report is treated as unverified until an authorized driver/admin confirms it.
- Emergency assistance is offered to suitable nearby active buses when the incident is confirmed and assistance is requested.

## 8. AI Transport Assistant
- The Transport Assistant answers questions using approved college transport documents through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
- RAG is intended for policy, procedure, FAQ, and other approved static transport information.
- Live GPS/ETA questions are handled by the tracking system rather than invented by the assistant.
- If approved documents do not contain an answer, the assistant should state that the available transport information does not provide the answer.

## 9. College-Specific Rules Requiring Approval
Before production use, the college should replace this template with approved values for:
- route service timings
- college arrival/deadline rules
- stop waiting rules
- holiday/service calendar
- transport office contacts/hours
- emergency procedures
- any route-change or passenger-access rules
