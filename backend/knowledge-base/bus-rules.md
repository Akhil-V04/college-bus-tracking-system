# College Bus Rules and Guidelines
**Document Type:** GUIDELINES  
**System:** College Bus Tracking System  
**Status:** TEMPLATE — replace with approved college rules

## 1. General Passenger Guidelines
- Follow the college's approved bus rules and instructions from authorized transport staff.
- Remain at the designated stop and follow safe boarding procedures.
- Do not obstruct the driver or distract the driver while the bus is moving.
- Report genuine transport problems through the application's issue-reporting feature or the college's approved reporting channel.
- Do not use emergency reporting for non-emergency complaints.

## 2. Driver Guidelines
- Use only the authenticated driver account issued by the administrator.
- Start tracking only for the authorized route/trip.
- Keep location permission and background tracking enabled while a trip is active.
- Follow the approved route unless an authorized diversion or safety situation requires otherwise.
- Do not interact with the mobile device in a way that compromises driving safety.
- Report breakdowns, accidents, GPS failures, or other operational problems through the approved procedure.

## 3. Tracking and ETA
- GPS and ETA information is provided to improve transport visibility.
- ETA values are estimates and may change.
- A stale, unreliable, or off-route state should be treated as uncertain rather than as a guaranteed arrival time.
- A passed stop should not display a negative ETA.

## 4. Issue Reporting
Available issue categories may include:
- Driver Behaviour
- Driving / Safety
- Bus Condition
- Route / Stop Issue
- Schedule Issue
- App Issue
- Other

Reports may be grouped by AI semantic similarity for administrative review. AI grouping does not establish the truth of a complaint.

## 5. Emergency Rules
- Use emergency reporting for genuine breakdown or accident situations.
- Do not submit repeated emergency reports for the same event unless there is a meaningful change.
- Follow official college safety instructions and emergency services where required.
- Drivers should only accept assistance tasks when it is safe to do so.

## 6. Privacy
- Passenger accounts are not required for the passenger experience.
- Private driver/admin contact information must not be exposed on public passenger screens.
- Passenger device location for nearby-stop lookup should not be retained unnecessarily.
- Transport records must be handled according to college privacy requirements.

## 7. College-Specific Rules to Replace This Template
The transport office should provide the final approved rules for:
- boarding/waiting procedure
- permitted passenger behaviour
- driver conduct and safety
- route deviation procedure
- emergency escalation
- service cancellation/holiday rules
- official contacts
- disciplinary consequences
