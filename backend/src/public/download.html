Built Specifically For College Commuters

Everything you need for a stress-free daily commute to and from campus.

🛰️

Live GPS Tracking

Watch your bus move along its route with real-time speed, live stop markers, and accurate time of arrival (ETA).

🌅

Morning & Evening Timetable

Clear separation between morning pickups to college and evening return trips with verified stop arrival and departure times.

📍

Bus Stops Near Me

Instant location-based stop detection. Finds all official college bus stops near your home and morning routes passing through them.

🔔

Smart Arrival Reminders

Set a custom reminder 1 to 5 minutes before the bus reaches your stop so you never rush or wait in the rain.

🛡️

Anonymous Issue Reporting

Report bus condition, driver behavior, safety, or timing issues directly to the transport department with 100% privacy.

🤖

AI Transport Assistant

Have questions about bus passes, routes, delays, or emergency contacts? Get instant verified answers 24/7.

1

Download APK

2

Allow Installation

3

Open & Track

Launch Campus Transit on your phone. All 25+ college routes are loaded and ready to track!
