## Document 1: Transport Policy (Type: POLICY)

**Title:** College Transport Policy

**Content to paste:**

College Transport Policy

1. Bus Pass Requirement
All students and faculty using college transport must possess a valid bus pass issued by the transport office. Bus passes are non-transferable.

2. Boarding Rules
Passengers must board only at their assigned boarding stop. Passengers must not board a bus assigned to a different route unless authorized by the transport office.

3. Timings
All buses depart from their first stop at the scheduled time. Buses will not wait beyond 2 minutes at any intermediate stop. The college gate closing time is 9:30 AM.

4. Conduct
Passengers must not distract the driver while the bus is in motion. Loud music, smoking, and consumption of alcohol are strictly prohibited. Passengers must remain seated while the bus is moving.

5. Belongings
The college is not responsible for lost belongings. Do not leave valuables unattended on the bus.

6. Route Changes
Route changes are only permitted at the start of each semester with prior approval from the transport office.

## Document 2: Emergency Procedures (Type: EMERGENCY)

**Title:** Transport Emergency Procedures

**Content to paste:**

Transport Emergency Procedures

1. Bus Breakdown
If the bus breaks down during the journey:
- The driver will safely pull over and turn on hazard lights.
- Passengers must remain seated inside the bus unless instructed to evacuate by the driver.
- The driver will report the breakdown through the mobile app.
- The transport office will arrange an alternative bus or assistance from a nearby route.
- Do not attempt to exit the bus on a highway or busy road.

2. Accident
In case of an accident:
- Stay calm and follow the driver's instructions.
- The driver will contact emergency services (112) and the transport office immediately.
- Do not move injured persons unless there is immediate danger (fire, submersion).
- The transport office will notify the college administration and arrange medical assistance.

3. Medical Emergency
If a passenger requires urgent medical attention:
- Inform the driver immediately.
- The driver will stop at the nearest safe location and call emergency medical services.
- If the bus is near the college, the driver may proceed directly to the campus medical center.

4. Fire
If you notice smoke or fire:
- Alert the driver immediately.
- The driver will stop the bus and open all doors for evacuation.
- Evacuate calmly using the nearest exit. Do not push.
- Move at least 50 meters away from the bus.
- Call 112 for fire services.

5. Reporting
All passengers can report emergencies through the Campus Transit app using the Emergency / SOS button. Driver-reported emergencies are treated as confirmed. Passenger-reported emergencies are verified by the driver or transport office before dispatching assistance.

## Document 3: FAQ (Type: FAQ)

**Title:** Transport Frequently Asked Questions

**Content to paste:**

Transport FAQ

Q: How do I track my bus?
A: Open the Campus Transit app, select your route number, and you will see the live bus location on the map when the trip is active.

Q: What if my bus is running late?
A: The app shows estimated arrival times (ETA) for each stop. If the bus is significantly late, class advisors of affected students are automatically notified by email.

Q: Can I change my route or boarding stop?
A: Route changes are permitted at the beginning of each semester. Contact the transport office with a written request.

Q: What if I miss my bus?
A: The college is not responsible for alternate transport if a passenger misses the bus. Please arrive at your stop at least 5 minutes before the scheduled time.

Q: How do I report a problem with the bus or driver?
A: Use the "Report an Issue" feature in the Campus Transit app. Select a category, describe the problem, and submit. Reports are anonymous.

Q: Is my personal information stored in the app?
A: No. The passenger app does not require login or any personal information. Your privacy is protected by design.

Q: What do I do if the bus breaks down?
A: Stay seated and follow the driver's instructions. The driver will report the breakdown through the app. An alternative bus will be arranged.

Q: How many buses does the college operate?
A: The college operates 31 route services covering various areas around Hyderabad.

Q: Who do I contact for transport-related queries?
A: Contact the Transport Office during working hours (9 AM - 5 PM) or use the app's Transport Assistant for policy questions.

## Document 4: Bus Rules (Type: GUIDELINES)

**Title:** Bus Rules and Guidelines for Passengers

**Content to paste:**

Bus Rules and Guidelines

1. Always carry your college ID and bus pass.
2. Be at your boarding stop at least 5 minutes before the scheduled departure time.
3. Do not stand near the door while the bus is moving.
4. Give priority seating to physically challenged students and faculty.
5. Keep the bus clean. Do not litter.
6. Do not eat inside the bus.
7. Use headphones if you want to listen to music or watch videos.
8. Do not hang out of windows or doors.
9. Report any suspicious activity to the driver immediately.
10. In case of emergency, use the emergency exit at the rear of the bus.
11. The driver has the authority to refuse boarding to anyone violating these rules.
12. Repeated violations may result in suspension of bus pass privileges.
