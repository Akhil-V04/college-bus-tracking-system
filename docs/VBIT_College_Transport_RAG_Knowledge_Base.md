    list all matches.

    for that route.

    needs confirmation from the transport office. Do not silently
    reorder or correct times.

    capacity, attendance, operating days, or evening schedule based on
    this document.

    questions when escalation is appropriate.
8.  Treat all timings as supplied schedule data, not live GPS
    information.
